# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to provide a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and focus on what benefits the community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents minpdf. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated.

Use GitHub's **Report content** feature for sensitive behavior or violations of GitHub policy. Non-sensitive project concerns may be raised in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/actiledev/minpdf/issues). Never post private information or sensitive evidence publicly.
